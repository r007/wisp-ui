## '@wisp-ui/react': minor

Add `Shape` and the shapes toolkit (`@wisp-ui/react/shapes`): a catalogue of 35 ready-made shapes, `RoundedPolygon`, and `Morph`, the exact feature-matched morph between any two of them, plus a spring and an SVG `Shape` component that morphs when its shape changes. LoadingIndicator now uses it instead of an approximation.
